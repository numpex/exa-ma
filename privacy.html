Privacy Notice (RGPD)

This page describes how we process personal data on the Exa-MA project website, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR/RGPD).

1. Data Controller

The Exa-MA website is managed by:

Université de Strasbourg - Cemosis
UFR Math-Info, 7 rue René Descartes
67084 Strasbourg Cedex, France

For data protection inquiries, contact the institutional Data Protection Officer (DPO):

dpo@unistra.fr
www.unistra.fr/protection-des-donnees

2. Data We Publish

2.1. Team & Project Information

We publish professional information about project members, including:

  • Names and professional affiliations

  • Job titles and roles within the project

  • Professional email addresses

  • Professional photographs

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) — transparency of publicly-funded research project and professional communication.

2.2. Event Photography

Group photographs from project events may be published on this website.

Legal basis: Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) — participants are informed at events and can request removal.

2.3. Recruited Personnel

We publish information about personnel funded by the project, including names, positions, work packages, institutions, and employment periods.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) — accountability for public research funding (France 2030 / ANR).

3. Hosting & Technical Data

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub may collect:

  • IP addresses

  • Browser information

  • Access timestamps

This constitutes a transfer to the United States under GitHub’s data processing terms and Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data

  • Rectify inaccurate data

  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")

  • Restrict processing

  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest

  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact:

christophe.prudhomme@cemosis.fr

4.1. Photo Removal Requests

If you appear in a photograph published on this website and wish to be removed, please contact us with:

  • The URL of the page containing the image

  • A description of which image or your position in a group photo

  • Your name for identification

We will process removal requests within 30 days.

5. Data Retention

  • Team information: Retained for the duration of the project and archival purposes

  • Event photos: Retained for project documentation and reporting

  • Technical logs: Managed by GitHub according to their retention policies

6. Complaints

If you believe your data protection rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority:

CNIL (Commission Nationale de l’Informatique et des Libertés)
3 Place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715
75334 Paris Cedex 07, France
www.cnil.fr
