The next *Exa-MA General Assembly* will take place in *Bordeaux from Monday 18 to Wednesday 20 January 2027*, at the Inria research centre in Talence.
The three-day meeting will bring together the consortium for scientific progress reviews, PhD and postdoctoral presentations, poster sessions, and discussions on the project's roadmap.

== Event details

* *Dates*: 18–20 January 2027, beginning with a welcome lunch on Monday at 12:00 and ending with a packed lunch on Wednesday at 12:45.
* *Venue*: Centre de recherche Inria, 200 avenue de la Vieille Tour, 33400 Talence, France (Bordeaux).
